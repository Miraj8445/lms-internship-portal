Step-by-Step Flow: From Career Goal to Internship Offer

Detailed walkthrough showing how the existing LMS seamlessly powers the new College & Internship discovery features.

1

College Onboards & Maps Student Roster

Admin Action

The College Placement Officer (TPO) verifies institutional credentials and uploads their student cohort roster. Students logging in with institutional emails are auto-linked with their college ID and academic department.

POST /api/v1/college/roster/sync { college_code: "NIT-TECH", students_count: 340 }
2

Student Sets Goal & Upskills in LMS

LMS Core

Student selects career path ("Full-Stack Developer"), follows the dynamically generated learning roadmap, completes interactive coding practice assignments, and earns cryptographically verifiable certificates upon course completion.

3

Company Registers & Posts Role with LMS Prerequisite Tags

Recruiter Action

Verified companies configure job requirements directly mapped to the LMS skill taxonomy (e.g. requires React.js Advanced + Node.js Microservices). Companies can open it to all students or target specific partner colleges.

POST /api/v1/jobs/post { title: "Backend Intern", stipend: 45000, lms_skills_required: ["Node.js", "PostgreSQL"] }
4

Real-time Skill Matching & Gap Recommendations

Matching Engine

The Matching Engine computes intersection vectors between student achievements and job requirements. If a student is at 75% match, the system suggests the exact 1 course needed to qualify for 100% eligibility.

5

1-Click Apply, TPO Live Tracking & Selection Pipeline

End-to-End Synergy

Student applies with their verified LMS portfolio. The Company recruiter sees verified coding assignment grades and can advance the student on the ATS Kanban board. The College TPO receives real-time updates for every stage (Applied, Interview, Offer) to seamlessly maintain NIRF compliance.
